# Market log

> Weekly state of the compute market. Newest entry first.
> Written each week by the owner of that week's brief, before the brief's pitch on Monday.
> Every number has a source and as-of date. Every price states unit and window.

## Entry template

```
## Week of YYYY-MM-DD

Prices
- <hardware>, <contract type>: <price> <unit>, <window> (<change vs last week>). Source: <source>, as of <date>

Events
- <what happened>. Source: <source>, <date>

Financing
- <deal, size, terms, collateral>. Source: <source>, <date>

Power
- <capacity, interconnect, policy news>. Source: <source>, <date>

What it means
- 1 to 3 sentences.
```
